# Design — 认识你自己 | Know Yourself

A locked design system for the rebuilt application. Product truth lives in
`PRODUCT.md`; this file owns the visual and interaction world.

## Direction

**Creative north star: a community broadsheet assembled one observation at a time.**

The interface borrows the hierarchy and material honesty of a small independent
publication without imitating distressed paper or retro decoration. Posts are
the issue. Assessments are recurring editorial instruments. Private drafts are
the writer's desk.

## Genre

Editorial social product: tactile, direct, text-led, and operationally clear.

## Macrostructure families

- **Front page:** full-bleed photo hero + four facet sections, each in its own layout family (two-column, reversed, inline pill strip, compact split).
- **Community and discovery:** masthead + editorial statement + creation desk + single reading column.
- **Focused tasks:** compact masthead + narrow workbench.
- **Libraries and operations:** index rail + working column.
- **Long-form content:** bounded reading column with marginal metadata.

## Theme

- `--color-paper`: warm cream, `oklch(97.8% 0.015 92)`
- `--color-paper-strong`: warm stock, `oklch(99.2% 0.008 92)`
- `--color-ink`: warm umber near-black, `oklch(23% 0.028 48)`
- `--color-muted-text`: warm grey, `oklch(45% 0.026 50)`
- `--color-accent`: deep clay, `oklch(48% 0.105 48)`
- `--color-teal`: sage, `oklch(47% 0.045 150)` — secondary information
- `--color-signal`: honey, `oklch(75% 0.1 78)`
- `--color-danger`: red, `oklch(50% 0.15 25)`
- Topics are four warm exposures of the same room: self clay `oklch(46% 0.105 45)`, feeling ember `oklch(50% 0.09 25)`, relating honey-earth `oklch(51% 0.07 70)`, living sage `oklch(47% 0.06 140)`.

The morning-light skin (2026-09-19): a warm cream page with warm umber ink.
Deep clay marks actions and current state. Sage identifies secondary
information. Honey is reserved for measured values — a golden pixel always
means "you are here". The palette reads as a lamplit everyday room rather than
simulated newsprint or a clinical instrument panel. The paper carries a faint
tooth — a tileable grain (baked by `scripts/generate-paper-grain.mjs`) laid
over every surface at ~3.5% opacity (4.5% in dark) through a fixed
`body::after`; material honesty, never distress (2026-09-26). Every
foreground/background pair clears 5.4:1 — verify with `node scripts/check-palette-contrast.mjs`
(29 pairs across light and dark).

The homepage carries a handmade cuteness layer (2026-09-20) on top of this
skin: a round CJK question voice, emoji at fixed decorative positions, sticker
badges, washi-tape tabs, and a watercolor hero wash. Every element is specified under
Signature components; none of them may drift onto other surfaces.

## Typography

- Display: Archivo variable, weights 600–700 in use (620 for section titles, 640 for headlines), upright only.
- Question voice: the system round CJK face (`YouYuan`/`幼圆`, weight 500) speaks in first person — it carries only the homepage hero question and the facet entry lines, then falls back through the display stack.
- Body: system sans with native CJK metrics.
- Data: IBM Plex Mono, only for scores, counts, dates, and question indices.
- Display tracking: `-0.025em` for Latin and `0` for Chinese.
- Body measure: 65–75 characters.
- Heavy 750–850 weights are prohibited: CJK falls back to system sans, where
  synthetic bold reads as chunky rather than editorial.

## Spacing and shape

- Four-point named spacing scale in `tokens.css`.
- Page width: 80rem maximum; primary reading column: 46rem maximum.
- Corners are large and soft (since 2026-09-19): controls `--radius-control` 0.875rem, cards `--radius-panel` 1.375rem, media `--radius-media` 1.125rem.
- Pills are reserved for filters, tags, compact statuses, and the single catalog CTA.
- Separation prefers rules and whitespace. Shadows are reserved for overlays or a genuinely lifted editor surface.

## Motion

- Active publication/filter strips settle with a short overshoot.
- Ordinary feedback uses color, opacity, and transforms under 240ms.
- Content is visible before animation.
- Route arrival is the one exception (2026-09-26), and it speaks a small
  vocabulary — the destination picks the gesture:
  result = **letter** (the answer unfolds from both edges toward the center,
  420ms); test detail = **turn** (the page swings in from its left edge,
  460ms); quiz = **focus** (content blooms out of one point, 480ms); journal
  piece = **curl** (peels open from its bottom-right corner, 500ms);
  everything else = **rise** (the quiet lift, 260ms). Hard loads never
  animate, and reduced motion skips every gesture entirely; all other motion
  still shows content first.
- Reduced motion removes movement without removing state.
- No pointer-driven page distortion (2026-09-26): the liquid ripple
  (LiquidDistort, 2026-09-19) was judged ugly by the owner and removed — the
  page must never warp under the pointer.

## Shared component grammar

- The masthead is a horizontal editorial rule, not a floating navigation card.
- Feed items are articles. Their typography and media determine their rhythm.
- Author, date, content type, badges, and moderation state share one metadata system.
- Inputs resemble writing fields with visible labels and complete recovery text.
- Empty states explain the missing material and offer one useful next action.

## Signature components

The handmade homepage layer (2026-09-20). These are front-page-scoped; other
surfaces must not copy them piecemeal — scattered reuse is how a system loses
its signature and starts looking generated.

- **Watercolor hero** (`press-home-lede`): baked warm washes — clay, honey,
  honey-earth, and sage with turbulence-roughened edges (`public/bg/hero-wash-light/-dark.webp`,
  regenerated by `scripts/generate-hero-wash.mjs`, `npm run hero:wash`) — bloom
  full-bleed behind the lede on transparent grounds; a gentle paper scrim
  (62% → 0%, bottom to top) keeps the question readable in both themes; the
  band closes on a 1px rule (2026-09-26).
- **Sticker badge** (`press-home-facet-index`): a 2.1rem circle in
  `--accent-soft` with a 2px `--paper-strong` edge, holding the facet emoji,
  tilted −5° on odd facets and +4° on even ones.
- **Washi-tape tab** (`press-home-facet-anchor::before`): one 3.2rem × 1rem
  strip of accent-washed paper across the anchor card's top edge — 2px
  radius, +3° tilt, 0.85 opacity. One per anchor card, never a repeating
  texture.
- **Round question voice** (`press-home-lede h1`, `press-home-facet-entry`):
  the round CJK face at weight 500 speaks in first person — hero question and
  facet entry lines only.
- **Mascot rain** (`press-mascot-rain`, 2026-09-26): about a dozen mascot
  stickers and two tiny hand-drawn shapes (heart, star — `public/stickers/`)
  drift down through the hero's morning sky on invisible lanes, each with its
  own speed, sway, and gentle tilt (never a full spin — the mascots must not
  hang upside down). Decorative only: aria-hidden, pointer-transparent,
  painted behind the lede text and faded by the paper scrim near the bottom.
  The random layout is generated client-side only; under reduced motion no
  sprite exists.
- **Catalog CTA capsule** (`press-home-catalog-cta`): the page's one pill
  call-to-action, an accent outline that fills with `--accent` on hover.

## Accessibility and privacy

- All controls remain keyboard operable with visible focus.
- Touch targets are at least 44 CSS pixels.
- Layouts work at 320, 375, 414, and 768px, at 200% zoom, and without motion.
- Chinese and English keep equivalent hierarchy and actions.
- Publication, draft, processing, hidden, unpublished, and deletion states are named before the user acts.

## Prohibited defaults

Each ban names the default it blocks and the do that replaces it.

- No bento dashboard, glass cards, gradient text, decorative mesh, fake browser chrome, or icon-tile feature grid. Do: separate material with 1px `--line` rules and `--paper-strong` surfaces; mark emphasis with `--accent-soft` washes.
- No diagnosis language, invented usage figures, testimonials, or clinical claims. Do: describe tendencies in plain language; every number shown is a real measurement set in the mono face.
- No decorative monospace, italic display headings, or duplicated heading kickers. Do: monospace only for scores, counts, dates, and indices; display headings stay upright; one kicker per block.
- No emoji as functional icons, status indicators, or body-text decoration. Do: emoji only at the fixed decorative positions named in Signature components (hero question, hero lede copy, facet badges and titles, catalog CTA, voice headings).
- No inaccessible hover-only actions or drag-only ordering. Do: actions stay visible on keyboard focus; ordering always offers a keyboard path.
- No runtime third-party fonts, analytics, or AI image generation. Do: system stacks plus locally served variable fonts; first-party aggregate events only.

## Iteration guide

1. Change one component at a time and reference token names, never hex values.
2. After any palette change, run `node scripts/check-palette-contrast.mjs` —
   the 29 foreground/background pairs must stay at 5.4:1 or better in both
   themes.
3. After any import or file-structure change, run `npm run check:registry`;
   a new runtime relative import must also be added to the compile allow-list
   in `scripts/test-storage.mjs`.
4. For skin trials, screenshot `design-preview.html` (it renders both themes)
   with a throwaway Edge profile (`--user-data-dir` on a fresh temp dir).
   Byte-identical screenshots mean a cached fake render, not a real one.
5. Local `npm test` only runs with `NODE_ENV` cleared.

## Known gaps

- `--accent-soft` is referenced by nine `refactor.css` rules (sticker badge,
  active toolbar buttons, and friends) but declared nowhere in the cascade —
  those wash backgrounds currently compute to transparent. The shipped
  sticker badge reads as a white ring on bare paper, which is the look that
  was approved. Declaring the token would change approved visuals — owner
  decision, not a silent fix.
- Homepage micro-interactions use literal 160/200ms while the motion tokens
  (`--duration-short` 180ms, `--duration-medium` 280ms) sit unused by them.
- The round question voice depends on a system font; devices without YouYuan
  fall back to the display stack, so the cute voice varies by platform.
- The hero washes are baked at 16:9; the portrait crop keeps the quiet text
  zone but trims the right-side blooms, and the far-left sage whisper may
  touch the question's first line — recheck if the composition moves.
- The washi-tape tab is positioned for the current 16–22rem anchor column;
  it does not adapt to other card widths.
